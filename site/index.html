A small dot in the menu bar keeps the rhythm while you read, type or wait on a call. No Dock icon, no windows to manage.

One steady pace

Every session runs at 5.5 seconds in and 5.5 seconds out, about five and a half breaths a minute. Nothing to set up.

A countdown to follow

Open the menu bar dot for a larger circle with a countdown for each breath and a count of the cycles you’ve finished.

Out of your way

Click the circle to start, click again to stop. Close the popover and the dot keeps pulsing for the rest of the session.
